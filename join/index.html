What we are, stated plainly

Most recruiting pages describe a brokerage you have no way to verify. Every fact in this section is checkable — against the New York Department of State licence record, or against this website itself.

Gadura Real Estate, LLC is a licensed New York real estate broker working out of an office at 106-09 101st Ave in Ozone Park, Queens. Our NYS Real Estate Principal Office licence number is 109926909. The supervising broker is Vinod K. Gadura. We are independent and family-owned — not a franchise and not a branch of a national brand. Seventeen agents currently have a profile on this website.

And what this page is not telling you

  • We do not publish our commission terms. That number comes out of a conversation with the supervising broker, because it depends on what you bring and what you need from us. If a published split is the thing that matters most to you, there are brokerages that advertise one, and that is a reasonable way to choose.
  • We are not claiming a training or mentorship programme here. If you want to know who specifically would work with you and how often, ask — and hold us to the answer you get.
  • We are not promising you leads. Any brokerage promising a licensed agent a volume of leads is making a claim it cannot keep.
  • We are not showing you production figures, awards or testimonials. We would rather publish nothing than publish something we have not verified.

What that does and does not mean

  • It means the infrastructure exists. You would not be joining a brokerage whose web presence is a business card and a phone number. For a market where we found competitor recruiting pages with no working form at all, that is worth saying.
  • It does not mean we are handing you leads. We will not put a number on what any of it produces for an individual agent, because we cannot honestly do that — and a brokerage that does is telling you something it cannot keep.
