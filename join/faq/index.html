The questions we actually get

Answered as honestly as we are able

Including the ones where the honest answer is that we are not willing to answer on a web page — and why.

If an answer below reads like a dodge, say so when you call. We would rather argue about it than have you assume.

Questions agents ask us

What is the commission split?

We do not publish it on this website. The terms depend on what you bring and what you need from the brokerage, so a single number here would be either meaningless or misleading. Ask, and you get a direct answer from the supervising broker early rather than after several meetings. If a published split matters more to you than anything else, there are brokerages that advertise one, and choosing on that basis is entirely reasonable.

Do you give me leads?

No, and we would be careful with any brokerage that says it will. Promising a licensed agent a volume of leads is a claim nobody can keep. We run a substantial consumer-facing website across Queens and Long Island; what that does or does not produce for any individual agent is not something we are willing to put a number on.

How many agents do you have?

Seventeen agents currently have a profile on this website. We are deliberately not turning that into a headline roster figure, because we have not re-verified every current affiliation to a standard we would publish. If the number matters to your decision, ask and we will tell you where it stands that day.

Who would train me?

We are not advertising a formal training programme or a mentorship scheme, because we have not documented one. What we would say is: ask who specifically you would be working with and how often, get a name, and weigh that answer against what other brokerages put in writing. A vague answer from us or from anyone else should count against them.

What marketing or technology do I get?

Ask what exists today rather than trusting a list on a web page. We are not presenting a CRM, a marketing budget, an agent-website product or an AI tool as an agent benefit, because we have not verified any of them to that standard.

What are your production numbers?

We are not publishing them. This website has a documented history of statistics being published without a source behind them, and the standard we now hold ourselves to is that a figure needs a real source, a retrieval date and a defined period, or it does not go up. We would rather you discount us for having no numbers than quote you one we cannot stand behind.

Can I bring my team?

Possibly, but we are not going to describe terms on a web page that have not been agreed. Team structures, brand treatment and broker-level arrangements are a direct conversation with the supervising broker. Use the form and say what you are proposing.

Do you take part-time agents?

Ask. It is a real question with a real answer, and it depends on circumstances rather than on policy we have published. Whether a brokerage accepts part-time agents is one of the genuinely local questions that national advice cannot answer for you.

What happens to my listings and my pending deals if I move?

Your listings sit with your current brokerage, and moving one needs your broker's consent, your seller's consent and new paperwork. Commission on a deal that is under contract but not closed is governed by your own agreement, not by any law or MLS rule we could find. We have written the whole thing up with sources, including which questions are settled by law and which by your contract.

What happens after I submit the form?

Your enquiry goes to our office inbox through the form service this site has used for some time. A licensed agent or the supervising broker follows up using the contact details you give. We are not going to promise you a response time, because this site removed response-time promises on purpose and we are not putting one back. If you would rather not wait on a form, call the office directly on (718) 850-0010.
