Questions to ask any property manager

  • What exactly is included in the fee, and what costs extra?
  • Who holds the rent and the security deposits, and in what kind of account?
  • How are repairs approved, and up to what amount can the manager spend without asking you?
  • How and how often will you see income, expenses and receipts?
  • Who answers a renter's emergency call at night and on weekends?
  • Will the same written screening criteria be applied to every applicant, including renters who pay with a housing voucher?
  • How can either side end the agreement, and with how much notice?
