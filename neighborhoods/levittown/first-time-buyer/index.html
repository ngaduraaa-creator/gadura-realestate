First-Time Home Buyer Guide: Levittown, Long Island

Nassau County · a starter-home market close to Queens

Levittown is one of the more attainable ways into Nassau County homeownership. Levittown is the original mass-produced American suburb, built by Levitt & Sons starting in 1947. Its housing stock is famously uniform — Cape Cods and ranches on modest lots — which keeps entry prices among the most attainable in Nassau and makes it a first-rung market for buyers priced out of Queens.

What you'll pay

Median sale prices run about $576,027 (Zillow home value, 2026), around $447 per square foot. With homes moving in ~29 days, first-time buyers need pre-approval in hand and a clear budget before touring.

Down-payment help

New York first-time buyers may qualify for SONYMA loans and down-payment assistance. See our SONYMA guide and down-payment assistance overview — several programs apply on Long Island, not just in NYC.

Commute

Levittown has no LIRR station of its own; commuters use the neighboring Hicksville, Wantagh, Bethpage, or Seaford stations, each a short drive away.

Schools

Levittown is served by the Levittown Union Free School District. In Nassau, the school district is baked into the price — confirm the district for any address before you fall in love with a house.
