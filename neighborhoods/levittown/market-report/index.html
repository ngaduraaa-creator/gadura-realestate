Levittown, NY Real Estate Market Report (2026)

Nassau County, Long Island · ZIP 11756 · updated 2026-07

Levittown is a Towns of Hempstead & Oyster Bay community in Nassau County. As of Zillow home value, 2026, the median sale price is $576,027 (+4.6% year-over-year), at about $447 per square foot. Homes are selling in roughly 29 days with about 2 offers each — a competitive market. Recent list prices have run around $749K.

Levittown is the original mass-produced American suburb, built by Levitt & Sons starting in 1947. Its housing stock is famously uniform — Cape Cods and ranches on modest lots — which keeps entry prices among the most attainable in Nassau and makes it a first-rung market for buyers priced out of Queens.

What kind of homes are here

The tax roll shows Levittown is dominated by single-family housing, which is why inventory is tight and well-kept homes move fast:

Getting here

Levittown has no LIRR station of its own; commuters use the neighboring Hicksville, Wantagh, Bethpage, or Seaford stations, each a short drive away.

Schools

Most of Levittown is served by the Levittown Union Free School District. School-district lines are a major driver of price in Nassau — verify the exact district for any specific address before you buy or price a sale.
