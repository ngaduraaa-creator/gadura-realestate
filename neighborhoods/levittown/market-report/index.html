Levittown is a Towns of Hempstead & Oyster Bay community in Nassau County. As of Zillow, 2026, the median sale price is $576,027 (+4.6% year-over-year), about $447 per square foot, with homes selling in roughly 29 days and about 2 offers each.

Levittown is the original mass-produced American suburb, built by Levitt & Sons starting in 1947. Its housing stock is famously uniform — Cape Cods and ranches on modest lots — which keeps entry prices among the most attainable in Nassau and makes it a first rung for buyers priced out of Queens.

What kind of homes are here

The tax roll shows Levittown is dominated by single-family housing, which is why inventory is tight and well-kept homes move quickly. Recent list prices have run around $749K.

Getting here

Levittown has no LIRR station of its own; commuters use the neighboring Hicksville, Wantagh, Bethpage or Seaford stations, each a short drive away.

Schools

Most of Levittown is served by the Levittown Union Free School District. School-district lines are a major driver of price in Nassau — verify the exact district for any specific address before you buy or price a sale.

How much are homes in Levittown right now?

The median sale price in Levittown is about $576,027 (+4.6% year-over-year, Zillow, 2026), roughly $447 per square foot, with homes selling in around 29 days.

Is Levittown a buyer's or seller's market?

Levittown is competitive — homes average about 2 offer(s) and sell in ~29 days, favoring sellers who price accurately. Buyers should have financing pre-approved before touring.

What is the housing stock in Levittown?

Levittown is overwhelmingly single-family: 14,077 one-family homes, 444 two-family, 200 with an accessory apartment, and 4 three-family homes on the 2025 assessment roll.
