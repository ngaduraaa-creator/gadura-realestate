Hicksville, NY Real Estate Market Report (2026)

Nassau County, Long Island · ZIP 11801 · updated 2026-07

Hicksville is a Town of Oyster Bay community in Nassau County. As of Redfin, May 2026, the median sale price is $779,533 (+5.1% year-over-year), at about $504 per square foot. Homes are selling in roughly 44 days with about 8 offers each — a competitive market. Recent list prices have run around $849K.

Hicksville anchors central Nassau around the Broadway Mall and the South Broadway corridor, which has grown into one of Long Island's largest South Asian retail and dining districts. Housing is overwhelmingly single-family, with postwar Capes and ranches alongside newer colonials.

What kind of homes are here

The tax roll shows Hicksville is dominated by single-family housing, which is why inventory is tight and well-kept homes move fast:

Getting here

Hicksville station is one of Long Island's busiest LIRR hubs, on the Ronkonkoma, Port Jefferson, and Oyster Bay branches — roughly 40 minutes to Penn Station and Grand Central.

Schools

Most of Hicksville is served by the Hicksville Union Free School District. School-district lines are a major driver of price in Nassau — verify the exact district for any specific address before you buy or price a sale.
