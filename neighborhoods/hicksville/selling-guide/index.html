What is my Hicksville home worth?

Online estimates are a starting point, not a listing price. The right number comes from recent, comparable Hicksville sales — same school district, similar lot and condition. With 10,957 one-family homes in Hicksville, comps are plentiful but block-to-block differences matter.

Pricing strategy

In a market moving in 44 days with multiple offers, accurate pricing out of the gate beats "testing high." Overpriced homes sit, then sell for less. We price to the comps and let competition work for you.

Seller closing costs in New York

Plan for broker commission (negotiable), NYS transfer tax, attorney fees, and any payoff or title costs. We give you a net-proceeds estimate before you list — no surprises at closing.

Multi-family & investor-owned

Hicksville also has 689 two-family and 71 accessory-apartment homes — often owned by investors for whom a sale and a 1031 exchange can be timed together. If that's you, ask about coordinating the sale with your next purchase.
